Final asset: `public/orbital-icon-white.png`. Launcher assets: `public/orbital-white-192.png`, `public/orbital-white-512.png`, `public/orbital-maskable-512.png`. Apple touch icon: `public/orbital-apple-touch.png`. Favicon: `public/orbital-favicon-white.png`.

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. Approved direction: flat white dumbbell at the center of a classic atom, midnight navy background, no satellite dots. Generated original remains in the image library; the project contains resized production assets. The maskable version adds safe padding for Android launcher shapes.

## Final generation prompt

Edit the latest monochrome flat Orbital icon. Replace the single diagonal satellite orbit with the CLASSIC ATOM SYMBOL: three clean intersecting elliptical orbital loops at three different angles, symmetrically arranged around the center. At the nucleus place a clear bold HORIZONTAL DUMBBELL silhouette with a straight handle and balanced chunky weight plates. The dumbbell replaces the atom nucleus. No dots, no electrons, no satellite balls, no circles used as particles anywhere. Dumbbell must remain easy to recognize; use navy negative-space separation around it so the orbital lines do not clutter the weights. All foreground shapes PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF), uniform full-bleed midnight navy background (#020617). Strictly flat two-color vector-style graphic: no gradient, shading, texture, perspective, 3D, glow, shadows or bevels. Refined balanced geometric logo, orbital lines thinner than dumbbell plates but thick enough at 48px. Center entire symbol within the central 68 percent safe area for a mobile launcher. One square app icon only, no rounded outer corners, no mockup, no text or lettering.
